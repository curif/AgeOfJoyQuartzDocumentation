## Share your own pack on the Internet Archive

Besides itch.io, a pack can be published on [archive.org](https://archive.org) as a free item, and players can list and download its cabinets from an AGEBasic program. Read [[Cabinets Marketplace in Internet Archive org]]. Players can browse, download and install those cabinets from inside the headset with the [[Cabinets Marketplace in Internet Archive org#The Cabinet Market get cabinets from inside the headset|Cabinet Market]].
